Koshari

Koshari is one of the most popular Egyptian dishes.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Macaroni
  • Lentils
  • Chickpeas
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Fried Onions

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the rice and lentils separately.
  2. Boil the macaroni until tender.
  3. Prepare the tomato sauce with garlic and spices.
  4. Layer the rice, lentils, and macaroni in a serving dish.
  5. Top with chickpeas, tomato sauce, and fried onions.

Ful Medames

Ful Medames is a traditional Egyptian breakfast loved by millions.

Ingredients

  • Fava Beans
  • Olive Oil
  • Lemon Juice
  • Garlic
  • Cumin

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the fava beans until soft.
  2. Mash the beans lightly with a fork.
  3. Add garlic, cumin, and lemon juice.
  4. Mix well with olive oil.
  5. Serve hot with bread.

Mahshi

Mahshi is vegetables stuffed with seasoned rice and cooked in tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Zucchini
  • Bell Peppers
  • Grape Leaves
  • Rice
  • Tomatoes

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash and hollow the vegetables.
  2. Mix the rice with tomatoes and spices.
  3. Stuff the vegetables with the rice mixture.
  4. Arrange them in a cooking pot.
  5. Cook in tomato sauce until the rice is fully cooked.

rice milk

Rice pudding is a dish made from rice and milk, and commonly other ingredients such as sweeteners, spices, flavourings and sometimes eggs. Variants are used for either desserts or dinners. When used as a dessert, it is commonly combined with a sweetener such as sugar.

Ingredients

  • cups cold water
  • cup uncooked white rice
  • Sugar
  • milk
  • Cream

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the rice in water until soft.
  2. Add the milk and stir continuously.
  3. Mix in the sugar and cream.
  4. Cook until the mixture becomes thick.
  5. Serve warm or chilled.
